A Simple Guide to Better Sleep

Good sleep is essential for your health, your mood, and your ability to focus during the day.

Most adults need between seven and nine hours of sleep each night. Try to go to bed and wake up at the same time every day.

If you often have trouble falling asleep, avoid coffee in the afternoon and keep your bedroom dark and quiet.
